# AI Guru — Mathlet Visual Template Referrals

AI Guru keeps the 17 physical reference videos in `ai-guru-reference-video-registry.json`. Additional concept-matched visual templates are registered in `ai-guru-visual-template-registry.json` from the public `htpu/mathlet` project.

These Mathlet entries are **visual teaching templates**, not videos that AI Guru must replay. The planner may use their visual grammar—graph construction, transformation, highlighting, parameter motion, geometric construction, distribution building, etc.—while generating original verified AI Guru visuals.
