# AI Guru V45 — Separate Mathematical Steps and Captions

Teaching sequence:
textbook reference → verified mathematical step → visual action(s) → short caption → TTS → next step.

The textbook paragraph stays in the reference pane. The whiteboard is reserved for
mathematical transformations, highlighting, construction and other visual actions.
Older materials without `mathSteps` remain compatible; their prose is shown in the
caption panel instead of being painted onto the whiteboard.

QA checks step completeness, verification, caption presence and visualId mapping.
